# Nick's Smoky Sumac Tomato Spread

### Ingredients

* 2-3 small Roma tomatoes (depending on size of tomatoes and size of pan)
	* can use other tomatos as well. Example: cherry tomatoes work nice, but will create a spread with a lot more skins it it.
* 2-3 tablespoons olive oil (adjust for tomato quantity, want to a thin layer covering the bottom of the pan)
* 1-2 tablespoons heavy cream (or to desired amount)
* Seasonings (to taste, but around a teaspoon each unless otherwise noted):
	* salt
	* sumac
	* smoked paprika
	* cayenne (1/4 teaspoon)
	* white sugar
	* worcestershire sauce (1/2-3/4 teaspoon)
	* black pepper (~1/4-1/2 teaspoon)


### Instructions

1. Heat the olive oil in a small skillet over medium-low heat 

2. Wash and rough chop the tomatoes. Add tomatoes to skillet once oil is hot. CAUTION: there will be splatter.

3. Salt the tomatoes in the pan. Sprinkle to coat them evenly.

4. Mix the tomatoes and let them break down and cook in the oil. They should break down into a sauce, but retain some chunks and strings of the tomatoes.

5. Add the sumac, smoked paprika, cayenne, sugar, and worcestershire sauce. Let cook for a few minutes, letting the water boil off and the flavors combine. Taste and adjust seasoning.

6. Add black pepper and heavy cream.

7. Mix and let cook for a few more minutes, or until the flavor and consistency are what you prefer.

8. Serve with toast. Eggs pair well too.

### Notes
1. This was inspired from shakshouka and can used with eggs as well.
