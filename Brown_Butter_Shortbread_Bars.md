# Brown Butter Shortbread Bars (Ted Lasso Biscuits)

**originally from https://www.gimmesomeoven.com/brown-butter-shortbread-bars/#wprm-recipe-container-82242**

Makes: 48 biscuits, depending on how you cut them
Original Author: Ali

* modified by Nick
* This is a double batch from the original recipe so the baking time needs to be dialed in depending on if you bake in a single 9x13 or two 8x8 trays since that will change the doneness of the overall bake

This 5-ingredient brown butter shortbread recipe is easy to make and wonderfully rich and delicious. This makes enough dough to fill a 9x13 inch baking tray.

## Ingredients

* 454g unsalted butter (2 cups or 32 tablespoons)
* 240g powdered sugar (2 cups)
* 1 ½ teaspoon fine sea salt
    * I use diamond crystal Kosher salt since it will be topped with flaky salt
* 3 teaspoons vanilla extract
* 4 tablespoons of water
* 568g all-purpose flour (4 cups)
* flaky sea salt (I've used fleur de sel and it also is great)


## Instructions

1. Brown the butter. Melt the butter in the large sauté pan over medium-high heat. Continue cooking until the butter begins to foam, stirring frequently to ensure that the butter cooks evenly. Once the butter solids begin to turn golden brown and the butter smells toasty, immediately pour it into a heat-safe bowl. (Keep a very close eye on the butter while it is browning so that it does not burn, since it can go from golden to burned very quickly.) Refrigerate the butter for 1 hour or until it solidifies into a soft consistency, similar to mayonnaise.

2. Prep the oven and baking pan. Heat oven to 300°F. Line a 9x13-inch baking pan with parchment paper.

3. Mix the dough. Transfer the chilled butter to the bowl of a stand mixer and add the powdered sugar. Using the paddle attachment, cream the butter and sugar together on medium-high speed for 2 minutes or until pale and creamy. Add in the salt, vanilla extract, 4 tablespoons of water, and beat briefly until combined. Add the flour and beat until just combined (do not overmix). 

4. Press dough into pan. Press the dough as evenly as possible into the prepared baking pan. Use a toothpick or a fork to poke holes all over the dough about 1 inch apart.

5. Bake. Bake for 50 to 55 minutes or until golden brown and lightly firm to the touch. Transfer the pan to a wire rack to cool for 20 minutes. Then carefully lift up the parchment and transfer the large bar to a cutting board. Slice into your desired size of bars and sprinkle with flaky sea salt. Can eat immediately or let cool further and then store in a container for overnight/long-term.
    * Dix Hills oven could go longer to create more color and browning on the base, probably closer to 60-65 minutes
    * 70 minutes yielded nicely browned bottom and very tasty biscuits, but the center of the bake was significantly lighter than the edges, so going even longer (~75-80 minutes) might yield even better results
    * Note: it's important to cut the bars while still warm since they can shatter easily when fully cooled
