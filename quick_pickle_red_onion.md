# Pickled Red Onions

**Original Source: https://www.loveandlemons.com/pickled-red-onions/**
**modified by Nick

## Equipment
10-oz. Mason Jars (or whatever jars you want to use)
Sharp kitchen knife
Medium Pot

## Ingredients

### Full batch
2 medium red onions
2 cups white vinegar
2 cups water
0.33 cup cane sugar
2 tablespoons sea salt

### 0.75x batch Ingredients
**Note: this is what I use for a single jar and a single large/medium onion)**
1 medium red onions
1.5 cups white vinegar
1.5 cups water
0.25 cup cane sugar
1.5 tablespoons sea salt

### 0.5x batch Ingredients
1/2 medium red onions
1 cups white vinegar
1 cups water
2.6 tablespoons cane sugar
1 tablespoons sea salt


### optional additives
* garlic cloves
* teaspoon mixed peppercorns
* red chili flakes

### Note: I like to layer peppercorns and chili flakes 2-3 times between the onions for my own quick pickle

## Instructions

1. Heat the vinegar, water, sugar, and salt in a medium saucepan over medium-high heat. Stir until a strong simmer and sugar and salt are dissolved. Set the pan off the heat for ~5 minutes.

2. Using a sharp, honed knife: thinly slice the onions, and divide the onions between 1-2 (16-ounce) jars or 3 (10-ounce) jars (depending on batch size). Place the optional additives (e.g. peppercorns and chili flakes) each jar, if using.

3. Carefully pour the brine into the jars, covering the onions completely. Use a spoon, butter knife, or chopsticks to gently agitate the onions and disrupt any air bubbles in the jar.

4. Seal the jar and place into the refrigerator immediately (can place on mat or buffered surface if you're concerned about heat shock). Should be ready to eat one the jar is cooled, about ~15-25 minutes.
